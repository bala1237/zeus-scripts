## home_cottage_t1 (starter cottage)

Item: a humble starter cottage for a new Olympian citizen, a cosy one-room single-storey Greek home with a rectangular footprint about one and a half times as wide as it is deep, warm cream plaster walls with slightly rounded hand-made edges, a low-pitched terracotta tile gable roof with a small plain triangular gable over the front, a small gold lightning-bolt emblem in the gable, a sturdy blue painted wooden door at the front centre, two small square windows with blue wooden shutters, a simple stone doorstep, one terracotta pot with a small olive shrub beside the door, a bronze wall lantern. Simple and lived-in but well made, clearly the first tier of a home that can later be upgraded.

Materials: cream plaster, terracotta tile, painted wood, rough stone, polished gold, bronze.

Concept A chosen (wide front, stone base). 3D: Trellis 2 chosen over Hunyuan 3D 3.1 Pro (both kept in models/ for reference). Normalised with `--fit 6.8,0,4.8 --yaw 0 --tris 30000 --tex 1024`.
