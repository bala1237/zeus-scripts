## How to send it

- Easiest: record each line as its own file named `01.wav`, `02.wav` ... `12.wav` (MP3 is fine too).
- Or one file with all twelve lines in order and about one second of silence between them; I will cut it.
- Record clean: no music, no effects. I will mix it over Mythic Dawn and duck the music under the voice.
- If a line runs long, I can shift a cut by a beat, but lines 1, 2 and 11 are the tightest.
