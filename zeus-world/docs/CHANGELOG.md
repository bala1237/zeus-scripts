# Zeus World: changelog

What changed, when and why. Newest first. One entry per piece of work, in plain language, with the backlog number where there is one. The commit history has the line-by-line detail.

## 26 Nov 2026

- **Open world, phase 1: life (ZW-051).** About 75 people now live in the world: sellers and buyers at the village stalls, people at the well and the shrine, farmers at the farmsteads, travellers on the road, townsfolk at the city gate. The story follows a newcomer who walks the road, stops on the hill for the first sight of the city, and arrives at the gate. A plan in nine phases is written (`docs/WORLD-PLAN.md`).
- **Hero film restarts when its source is shown again (ZW-050).** The owner found that hiding and showing the source in OBS did not start the film again. A film opened by the OBS link now stops and rewinds when hidden and plays from the start when shown.
- **Hero film: a link for OBS, and a line to crop by (ZW-050).** The hero page has a new button, Copy link for OBS: it copies an address holding the chosen film and name, with the controls off and the film starting by itself, to paste into a browser source. For a window capture instead, a thin gold line now marks the 16:9 picture while the controls are showing.
- **Open world: the jitter on fast low flights (ZW-049).** The cause was the camera's own path: the road and river were stored as straight pieces, so the camera's lean and heading jumped at every joint. They are now true curves. The lean changes by under 0.3 degrees a frame where it jumped by up to 18. A test now guards every shot.
- **Open world: fixes for jerky motion (ZW-049).** The owner found the camera's movement jittery. Hidden pixels no longer cost as much as visible ones, the picture is drawn at 1.25 times the screen instead of twice, new land enters a little per frame, shadows are redrawn on alternate frames, and there is less heavy detail in view. Not confirmed smooth until he checks.
- **A forest kit for the open world, and a Greek village (ZW-049).** The catalogue had no forest tree. Nine new models made the city's way: holm oak, stone pine, olive, plane, mountain fir, laurel, ferns, broom, fallen log. The forest now uses them, in full detail close up and as photographed cards far off, so it is thick to the horizon. Grass, flowers and rocks come from the catalogue too. The village is rebuilt from the city's villa parts round a paved square with banners.
- **The open world, first proof (ZW-049).** A new page, `/world.html`: a land that goes on without edge, with the city by the sea, a river that falls from a cliff and runs through forest and farmland, a village, and mountains in the west. The camera tells a two-minute story with ten kinds of move (drone, crane, hand-held, fast low flight and others), and seven buttons fly it to places. No people yet, and the city is a stand-in.

## 25 Nov 2026

- **The Fire-Bringer without children (ZW-047).** Its faces shot, used in the opening and after the climax, is made again with adults only and the film rebuilt. All three hero films now follow the rule.
- **No children in the hero films (ZW-047).** The owner's rule. The Well's two pictures with children are replaced with adults and the film rebuilt.
- **Two new hero films: The Last Banner and The Well (ZW-047).** The Last Banner is for a battle that was lost: one of us raises the fallen banner and by it everyone finds the way home. The Well is for generosity: one of us walks into the desert with an empty jar and brings back water for all. Each is about 46 seconds, made the cheaper way (two moving shots, the rest still pictures the film moves across), about $2.22 for both. No gender in either; the hero is only seen from behind or with the face covered.
- **The hero's picture on the end card, and two new scripts (ZW-047).** A picture can be chosen, pasted or dropped on the hero page; it shows in a gold ring inside the laurel and is sent to OBS with the name. The page can now hold several films. Scripts written for The Last Banner (a lost battle) and The Well (generosity); not yet made.
- **A remote for the hero film, Zeus World without controls, tempo in the laser address (ZW-048).** The hero page in Chrome can now play the film inside OBS with the name typed there ("Play in OBS"), through OBS's built-in remote control. `city.html?ui=0` shows Zeus World with nothing over the picture. `bpm=128` in the laser address sets its tempo when it is not listening.
- **The laser page reconnects to its sound input (ZW-048).** If the input goes dead or the computer changes its sound devices (headphones connecting does this), the page takes the input up again by itself instead of staying silent.
- **A status line for the laser page without controls (ZW-048).** `debug=1` in the address shows, on the picture, whether it is on the beat, the tempo, which sound input it hears and a lamp that blinks on the beat. For checking an OBS browser source.
- **The laser page can listen to a sound input (ZW-048).** Besides sharing a browser tab, it can now hear the music from a sound card or mixer such as the owner's RODECaster, or the microphone. A new button with a list of inputs, and `listen=input` in the address so that a controls-off page starts listening by itself.
- **The Fire-Bringer, second cut (ZW-047).** After the owner's review. A new opening that hides what the film is about (faces looking up at something not shown), a low point before the climax, and the opening answered at the end. A new, authoritative narrator. The score added, with the fire erupting on the music's lift and the music stepping down under the voice. The name rebuilt as a film title: bevelled gold letters forged out of a streak of light, with the honour beneath, a glint and embers. No new footage was generated.
- **The first hero film: The Fire-Bringer (ZW-047).** A 63-second story film that celebrates a battle's top giver: the city's fire is dying, one hooded figure carries a flame up the mountain through the storm, the great fire erupts and the city is lit from it. The person's name is put in when it plays: forged in gold over the fire, cut in stone, on the end card, and spoken by the narrator when a recording for that name exists (one does for Andie Howard). New page `/hero.html`. Six generated shots (about $3.00) and narration in the owner's own voice; the score is generated but not yet added. `assets/review/hero.html`, `hero.js`, `herofilms.js`, `assets/hero-build/`.
- **Pegasus, with a staging of his own (ZW-046).** No lightning: he gallops in across the clouds while hoofbeats of silver light run along the pillars, and when he spreads his wings the stage's lasers open into two wings of white light and feathers fall. A third generated clip (12 s, about $0.60).
- **Zeus, with a staging of his own (ZW-046).** For TikTok's Zeus gift: his eyes open in the dark above the temple, which stands as a black silhouette; he raises a thunderbolt and throws it; the stage's own bolt lands on the temple where the clip's comes down; lightning runs along the pillars and lights all twelve flames. A second generated clip (14 s, about $0.70).
- **A realistic lion for the largest gifts (ZW-046).** The owner wanted the gift as realistic 3D animation and approved a small spend. A ten-second clip of a lion walking out of a storm and roaring was generated (about $0.50) and now plays as the whole sky behind the temple for lion-family gifts of 5,000 coins and more, with the stage's strike, name and film bars around it. Also fixed: the lasers had stopped stepping back during gifts.
- **The largest gifts play as a cinematic show that grows with the gift (ZW-046).** Film bars and a title, six acts each with its own camera, the figure waking with lit eyes and breathing, and three sizes by coins: the bigger ones add an aurora, meteors, a web of lightning across the sky and every pillar struck. Up to about 26 seconds.
- **The largest gifts become figures in the sky (ZW-046, first one).** TikTok has 93 gifts of 5,000 coins or more; they are grouped into ten families. A gift that large now has its figure drawn above the temple as a constellation, by lightning, which then wakes. The lion family is built as the proof; the rest play the big moment until drawn. `assets/review/giftcatalog.js`, `constellations.js`.
- **Gift moments redesigned (ZW-045).** The owner asked for "professional creativity" that makes people say wow. Every gift is now lightning: a small one strikes a pillar and leaves a flame burning; a medium one leaps along the whole row; a big one gathers a storm, strikes the temple, writes the name in molten gold and rains gold. When all twelve flames burn, the temple answers by itself. `assets/review/giftfx.js`, `gifts.js`.
- **Gift moments, fired by test buttons (ZW-045).** On the laser stage: a small gift brings lightning onto one pillar and the sender's name onto the booth; a medium one a run of light along all the pillars; a big one a beat of black, then lightning, every pillar lit, the big laser pattern and the name in lights. Gifts queue and land on the beat. Real gifts from TikFinity are to be connected later. `assets/review/gifts.js`.
- **Laser page: the picture in a resizable 16:9 box, controls below (ZW-044).** The same layout as Andie's page, at the owner's request. It also gains `ui=0` for use as a browser source and keeps running when its window is covered.
- **Andie's Place did not animate in TikTok LIVE Studio (ZW-043).** A page with its controls off now starts by itself; the show keeps running when the browser stops drawing a covered window; the build suits older built-in browsers. New "Copy link for LIVE Studio" button, and `debug=1` to see what the page is doing. Not yet confirmed in LIVE Studio.
- **Andie's Place: the picture is a resizable 16:9 box in the middle of the page (ZW-042).** At the owner's request, so there is space round it. A Size slider and a draggable gold corner; the size is remembered.
- **Andie's Place: picture in a fixed 16:9 frame, controls below it (ZW-042).** At the owner's request. Nothing is cropped any more and the controls never lie over the picture; H hides them.
- **Andie's Place (ZW-042).** A stream backdrop for Andie Howard, who plays the owner's songs on her stream: her black, gold and purple studio rebuilt in code, whose own neon follows the music (chase, flashes, fill, strobe), with an optional layer of purple and gold lasers, a brightness control, an editable welcome sign, and every setting available in the address for a TikTok LIVE Studio browser source. `/andie.html`; `knowledge/andie.md`.
- **The reference video watched, and the building cues rebuilt (ZW-041).** The owner uploaded the Transmission video and it was read frame by frame. The stage is now what the video has: a temple of six pillars with three free-standing pillars each side and rows of warm bulbs, lit in amber, red and gold. A new cue, oracle, reproduces the opening: short flashes with black between, jumping from the temple to the side pillars to single pillars. During building cues the view cuts between wide and close. The video's timeline is written up in `STAGE-PLAN.md`.
- **Building cues for Olympus (ZW-041).** The owner described what the video does that Olympus lacked: blackouts in which the building is lit, pillar by pillar. Four cues with the lasers off: a light chasing along the pillars and back, a corner pillar blinking, the colonnade filling from each side, and the whole building strobing (held to twice a beat for photosensitive viewers). Each pillar now has its own lamp.
- **Buttons on the laser page could not be clicked (ZW-040).** The picture lay on top of the control panel and took the clicks. The panel now sits above it. A new check, `assets/clicktest.mjs`, clicks every button with a real pointer.
- **The festival look, Olympus (ZW-039).** The owner sent the Transmission Prague laser show as "something i loved". A seventh theme now opens the show: a wall of ten projectors throwing fans of twenty green beams each, magenta shafts, five patterns of its own, a temple stage with ZEUS on the booth and a laser lattice behind. A Stage set button leaves only the light. `assets/review/director.js`, `laser.js`.
- **New beat tracker (ZW-038).** The owner found the beat capture "not perfect". The listening was rebuilt: it measures bass, mids and highs 200 times a second off the audio itself, finds the tempo and where the beat falls, holds them through parts with no drums, and fires the lights on the predicted beat. A Beat timing slider corrects any delay. The show goes quiet in a breakdown and fires its biggest pattern on the drop. Both the laser page and the stage use it. `assets/review/tracker.js`, `beat.js`; 15 tests on made-up music.
- **Flow mode, first version (ZW-037).** The owner asked for an "infinite move" version and sent three reference videos. From the first of them: ribbons of coloured silk that wind and twist for ever on black, on the laser page under the Show button, alone or behind the lasers, in the theme's colours and following the music. `assets/review/flow.js`.
- **Lasers in depth, with a director, themes and camera moves (ZW-036, phase 3).** The owner passed the prototype and asked for more 3D, themes and cinematic camera movement. `/laser.html` now has seven projectors at different distances, a Lighting Director that plays ten patterns under rules (no repeats, darkness before the big moments, never the whole rig at once outside them), six colour themes, and a view that glides between six positions. Blackout and Next look buttons. `assets/review/director.js`, `laser.js`; tests in `assets/test/`, run with `npm test`.
- **Laser prototype (ZW-036, phase 2).** One projector as an overlay at `/laser.html`: beams and scanned sheets drawn as light in drifting haze, with white-hot cores, a glow that only the beams trigger, and movement under speed limits. The existing stage is unchanged. `assets/review/beams.js`, `laser.js`.
- **Brief received and audit written for a professional laser and lighting system (ZW-036).** `STAGE-PLAN.md` holds the owner's brief, an assessment of the current stage, the causes of its artificial look, a phased plan and five questions. Nothing built.

## 22 Nov 2026

- **Research for the game direction (ZW-023).** Five strands: what makes open worlds hold people, earlier audience-driven games, retention and its pitfalls, AI-driven living worlds, TikTok LIVE's mechanics and rules. Report in `reports/` at the repository root; notes in `research_notes/`. Nothing built.
- **The bigger plan written.** `ROADMAP.md` sets out the game in six phases, from the owner's direction: a palace with queens (ZW-020), a living day for long streams (ZW-021), walking through the battle MVP's property (ZW-022), and building it like an actual game in phases (ZW-023). Nothing built.
- **Cinematic camera for the battle (ZW-019).** The owner approved the siege and asked for a closer, film-like camera. Nine kinds of shot now cut between the clash, the ranks, the catapults, the wall and the gate, following the fighting line. Soldiers gained legs and helmets for the closer views.
- **Siege at the south gate (ZW-018, first version).** During a battle two armies face each other outside the wall and the line between them moves with the score, with arrows, catapults, a rival camp and a strength bar. Runs on a made-up score from the Battle button or `?battle`. `assets/review/battle.js`.
- **Game direction logged and brainstormed.** ZW-015 (losing a battle damages a building, the community repairs it), ZW-016 (winning triggers a surprise celebration) and ZW-017 (make the city feel like a game: levels that unlock things, a world to explore). Ideas written up in `BRAINSTORM-GAME.md`. Nothing built.
- **Each frame now costs about half as much to draw (ZW-004, first batch).** Repeated pieces (tiles, walls, river and quay sections) are drawn in one go; citizens outside the view are skipped; the man model was three times heavier than the others and is now the same; distant citizens, trees and bushes use lighter far-view models. Wide view: 2,330 to 1,154 draw calls, 5.0 to 2.2 million triangles. Frame rate itself is for the owner to read on the preview.
- **Tools added:** `assets/lod.mjs` and `far.sh` make the far-view models; `measure.mjs` now lists what each model costs in the scene.
- **Citizens stand still properly (ZW-014).** The supplied idle animation was a wide, turning stride. A standing pose made in code replaces it for all three citizens: feet together, arms hanging, breathing, a slow look from side to side. `assets/review/stand.js`.
- **The woman citizen walks properly (ZW-012).** Her rig from the generator had her upper body and head bound to her left forearm, so she folded and twisted as she walked. Her binding was rebuilt from her shape with a new tool, `assets/reskin.mjs`, at no cost. Checked with frame sheets of all four of her animations.
- **Homes show their owner's name (ZW-013).** A plate above each finished home's roof, readable from the wide view. `assets/review/names.js`. Names are made up for the demo.
- **Frame sheets for checking animation:** `assets/review/poses.html` draws a character at evenly spaced moments of a clip, since motion cannot be watched on the build machine.
- **Memory use of the city roughly halved (ZW-001).** Measured first: 1.4 GB in the tab and 2.1 GB in the graphics process, with no leak. Two causes: the same textures loaded many times over (1,065 in memory, 144 different), and a 512 MB shadow map. Identical textures are now loaded once, and the shadow map is stored in 192 MB. After: 0.7 GB and 0.8 GB. The picture is unchanged, checked against stills of four views. Detail in `knowledge/performance.md`.
- **Measuring tool added:** `assets/measure.mjs` reports what the city holds in memory, what a frame costs to draw, and whether anything grows over time. New skill `measure-memory`.
- **Frame cost measured (ZW-004):** 5.0 million triangles and 2,320 draw calls a frame. Not yet worked on.
- **Documentation system set up.** Added `CLAUDE.md` (read by every session), `PLAN.md`, `BACKLOG.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, this changelog, the `knowledge/` folder and five skills in `.claude/skills/`. `STATUS.md` was split into these and removed. Why: so any new session has everything it needs without being told where to look, and so reports from the owner are tracked.
- **Backlog seeded** with ZW-001 to ZW-011: the owner's Chrome memory report and the outstanding items from the old status file.
- **Merged into the default branch.** The work from `claude/dazzling-mendel-y9qquo` and `claude/eager-faraday-svkb22` had never been merged, so new sessions could not see it.
- **City scene: day-to-dusk cycle and camera tour** (batch 5). Two photo skies cross-fade, lamps come on, the sea calms in the evening; the camera glides between seven stops.
- **City life batch 3, ambient motion.** Rippling cloth, swaying trees, chimney smoke, fountain spray, a sailing boat, drifting sky.
- **City life batches 1 and 2.** Walking citizens on a route graph; buildings rising behind scaffolding with Bolts labels; lightning and cheering on completion; birds.
- **Stream grade.** Deeper sky and grass, harder sun, saturation and contrast boost, because the first look was too pale for OBS.
- **Water and extras** added to the catalogue (pond, river, stepping stones, quay, pier, well, reflecting pool), all scripted.
