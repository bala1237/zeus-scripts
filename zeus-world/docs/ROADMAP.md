# Zeus World: the bigger plan

How the city grows from a showpiece into a game people stay for, built in phases. Written 22 Nov 2026 from the owner's direction. It is a plan to improvise on, not a contract: phases can be reordered, split or dropped. The ideas behind it are in `BRAINSTORM-GAME.md`; the numbered work is in `BACKLOG.md`.

## What the owner asked for

- "We need a palace and have the queens in there. I will get to choose who they are. For now we can have random."
- "Since it's going to be during my stream which runs for long hours, we need to ensure we have a lot more things people can explore and we showcase. People working, markets, community celebrations, dancing and so on."
- "After battle, I should be able to walk through the property of the MVP, in and out, and showcase what they have built for themselves."
- "I need help with ideas and expanding on it to build like an actual game. We don't have to do everything now, but have the bigger plan and build in phases and improvise."

## The shape of the game

Three loops, each feeding the next:

| Loop | Length | What happens | Why people stay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The moment | Minutes | Something is always going on: work, a market, a dance, a procession, a build rising | There is always something to look at, and the camera finds it |
| The stream | Hours | A programme of events through the stream, battles with a fight and an aftermath, showcases of people's homes | "What's next?" has an answer every few minutes, and a big one every hour |
| The city | Weeks | The city levels up, new places open, mysteries are revealed, seasons turn | Coming back shows something new, and their own part in it |

Around those: each person's own place in it (a home, a name, a title, things collected), and Zeus directing it all from a console.

## Phases

Each phase ends with something the owner can show on stream. Costs are noted where a phase needs paid generation; everything else is code and scripted models.

### Phase 1: a living day (ZW-021)

The city has things happening in it all the time, and a programme so a long stream never runs dry.

- **People at work.** The smith hammers at the forge, the baker carries trays, the potter turns a wheel, fishermen haul nets on the quay, builders carry blocks, farmers bring carts through the gate, a teacher with children outside the library.
- **The market.** Market day fills the plaza: more stalls, crowds, haggling, porters with baskets.
- **Celebrations.** A dance in a ring in the plaza, a procession up the sacred way to the temple, a play in the amphitheatre with an audience, athletes racing at the gymnasium, a wedding, a feast under the pergolas.
- **The city's programme.** A clock of events: something small every ten to fifteen minutes, something big every hour, announced on screen a little ahead ("The dance begins at the plaza in 2 minutes").
- **The showcase camera.** The film-style camera built for the battle, generalised: when an event starts the camera goes to it with close shots, then returns to the tour.

How: movements made in code where they can be (hammering, carrying, bowing, a ring dance), as the standing pose was. Paid animations only where code is not good enough.

### Phase 2: the palace and the queens (ZW-020)

- **The palace.** A new civic building, scripted like the others: a grand courtyard, a balcony over the plaza or the sacred way, a throne hall seen through open colonnades.
- **The queens.** Crowned figures who live there. Chosen by the owner; random for now. They appear on the balcony, walk in the gardens with attendants, and take part in events: opening a festival, crowning a champion, blessing a victory.
- **The court.** Palace guards, attendants, a herald who announces events.
- First version free: the existing woman citizen with a crown and a royal colour. Distinct queen models later would be paid generation.

### Phase 3: homes worth visiting (ZW-022)

- **The showcase walk.** The camera walks a visitor's path through one person's property: through the garden gate, round the garden, up to the porch, with their name on screen. Usable any time ("take me home"), and after a battle for the battle's champion.
- **Inside.** Then through the door: a room furnished from the catalogue. This is new ground: the project has been exteriors only until now, and interiors need rooms, furniture and a way for owners to arrange them.
- Step one is the walk outside and up to the door. Step two is one furnished room per home tier. Step three is rooms the owner arranges in the phone builder.

### Phase 4: levels and unlocks (ZW-017)

- A shared city level with a name, a bar on stream, and a level-up moment.
- Locked things visible in the city: sealed plots, silhouettes, a shut gate.
- The palace, the market day, the dances and the rest become things a level opens, so the phases above turn into the ladder.
- The four teaser mysteries as the big reveals.

### Phase 5: battles, complete (ZW-015, ZW-016, ZW-018)

- Real TikTok battles drive the siege: five minutes, the score dictating the action.
- A loss leaves damage and opens a repair. A win triggers a surprise celebration.
- The champion's home is showcased afterwards (Phase 3).

### Phase 6: a world to explore (ZW-017)

- Mist beyond the walls that levels push back; expeditions on the trireme; the vault with many locks; hidden things to spot; seasons with a finale; a rival city across the water.

## Foundations, alongside the phases

These are not showpieces, but the phases lean on them.

| Foundation | Why | Backlog |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The director's console | The owner needs buttons: start an event, choose the queens, pick a home to showcase, start or stop a battle, send a storm | New |
| Real events | Bolts, viewers, pledges and battles from TikTok instead of made-up numbers | ZW-009 |
| The phone builder | Where people build their own plot. It still uses the old parts | ZW-009 |
| Performance | A quality setting for phones; lighter buildings. More activity means more to draw | ZW-004 |
| Sound | The city is silent. Ambience, a market's murmur, battle noise and festival music would do as much for the feeling of a game as any model | New |
| Saving the world | The city has to remember levels, queens, homes and history between streams | Constitution, "Persistence" |

## Suggested order

1. **Phase 1, a living day**, with the showcase camera and the programme. It answers the most pressing need, long streams, and everything after it plugs into the programme.
2. **Phase 2, the palace and queens.** A visible centrepiece, and free in its first version.
3. **A first director's console**, small: buttons for the events, the queens and the battle.
4. **Phase 3, step one**: the showcase walk outside.
5. **Phase 4, levels.**
6. Then battles complete, interiors, and exploration, with real events connected as soon as the capture path is confirmed.

## Things to settle

1. **The queens.** How many? What makes someone a queen: purely the owner's choice? What do they get and do? Is the palace theirs alone?
2. **Naming the battle's MVP.** The constitution says no ranking by spend may appear on stream or in narration. An MVP is the top supporter of a battle, so showcasing them by that name is a ranking of one. TikTok shows the MVP itself, so it may be fine to the owner, and it is the owner's rule to change. Options: allow it for battles only; or call them the "champion" and choose them on taking part as well as support.
3. **Interiors.** The standing decision is exteriors only. Walking inside reverses it. How far: a look through the door, one furnished room, or rooms people arrange themselves?
4. **Paid animations.** Dancing and working can be made in code for free, but will look simpler than bought animations. Is there a budget if the free versions are not good enough?
5. **Sound.** Wanted, and when?
6. **Which phase first**, if not the order above.
