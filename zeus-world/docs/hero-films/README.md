# Hero films

Short story films that celebrate the people who carried a battle (ZW-047). Played after the battle, after the owner has spoken and thanked them: for the top one, or for the top three as a team.

## The owner's rules (25 Nov 2026)

- It is a **story**, with a script like a film. Not "an AI video of the MVP", which everyone does.
- **Generic**, with the person's name put in when it plays.
- A **library** of scripts, so it is not the same film every time.
- A **strong hook**, a **heroic moment**, an **emotional** turn, and a **song or score** (Suno).
- **60 seconds. A narrator. Epic in tone.**
- From the constitution: honour the act, never the amount. No amounts and no ranking by spend on screen.

## How every script is built

1. **The hook** (about 5 s): black, and one line.
2. **The need:** what is at stake.
3. **The choice:** one of us steps forward.
4. **The heroic moment:** the deed, the name, the music breaking open.
5. **The honour:** what their act did for everyone, and the name set in stone.

The hero is **never seen from the front**: from behind, hooded, or in silhouette, so that the figure can be anyone. Narration says "one of us", "they", "theirs", never "he" or "she". The name appears where the script says `[NAME]`.
