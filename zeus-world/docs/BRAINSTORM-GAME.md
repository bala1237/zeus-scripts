# Brainstorm: make the city feel like a game

Backlog: ZW-017. Started 22 Nov 2026. This is a list of ideas to choose from, not a plan. Nothing here is decided or built. The phased plan that grew out of it is `ROADMAP.md`.

## The brief, in the owner's words

"What we have is the bare minimum. It should keep people glued to see what happens next."

"We need to bring in the gaming kind of approach. Different levels lock different things. That way people get motivated. That's how games work. It's just a community-driven game and not a single person playing."

"Let's brainstorm on making this game interesting where people want to see and explore the world."

## The core idea: the city levels up, and everyone can see what is locked

One shared level for the whole city, raised by the community together. Each level unlocks things, and the locked things are visible before they open, so there is always something to want.

**City levels.** A ladder with names, for example Camp, Village, Town, City, Polis, Capital, Olympus. Reaching a level is a live event with its own moment on stream.

**What raises the level.** A mix, so no single kind of viewer carries it: Bolts pledged to community builds, number of citizens, buildings finished, battles won, days the city has been alive.

**What a level unlocks.**

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| New places | The harbour, the acropolis, a second district beyond the wall |
| The four teaser mysteries | Colossus, sky temple, harbour, vault: one per major level, each a big reveal |
| Community builds | Stadium, lighthouse, aqueduct, each only offered from a certain level |
| Items for personal plots | New roofs, porches, a second storey, statues, garden pieces |
| Life | Animals, market days, boats, new citizen jobs |
| World features | Night, weather, seasons, festivals |

**Locked but visible.** This is what makes people look around:

- Empty plots with a sealed marker showing the level that opens them.
- Silhouettes or scaffolding outlines of buildings not yet unlocked.
- A gate in the wall that is shut, with something glimpsed behind it.
- Items in the phone builder shown greyed out with "opens at Town".
- A bar on stream at all times: the level, how far to the next, and what the next one opens.

## Ideas for wanting to explore

1. **Mist beyond the walls.** The land outside the city is covered in mist. Each level pushes it back and reveals something: a ruin, a grove, an island, a road to somewhere.
2. **Expeditions.** The community sends the trireme out. It is gone for a set time and comes back during a stream with a discovery: a new item, an animal, a piece of a map. Something to come back for.
3. **The vault with many locks.** A sealed vault in the city. Each lock opens on a different community feat (a hundred citizens, ten builds, three wins in a row). Nobody knows what is inside until the last lock.
4. **Hidden things.** Small secrets placed in the city: a cat on a roof, a carving, a door that is sometimes open. The first viewer to spot one in chat is named on it.
5. **"Take me home."** A chat command sends the camera to that viewer's house. A reason to build something worth showing.
6. **What's new tour.** At the start of each stream the camera visits everything that changed since last time.
7. **A map that grows.** A drawn map of the world that fills in as places are found, shown between scenes and shareable.

## Ideas for "what happens next"

8. **Tonight's quest.** Every stream has one clear goal with a payoff before the end, for example lighting the lighthouse lamp.
9. **Cliffhanger.** The stream ends with something appearing: sails on the horizon, smoke from the mountain, a stranger at the gate. The next stream starts with the answer.
10. **Seasons.** Four to six weeks with a theme, a season build, and a finale. The season's monument stays forever.
11. **Zeus acts.** The streamer is Zeus. From the director console he can send a storm, a blessing, a golden hour, a challenge. Unpredictable, and it makes him part of the game.
12. **Votes.** The community chooses the next district, the next build, the name of a street. The constitution already allows for civic votes.
13. **Battles.** Losing damages a building and opens a repair (ZW-015). Winning triggers a surprise celebration (ZW-016). A winning streak makes the celebrations rarer and bigger.
14. **A rival city, and a battle you can watch.** During a TikTok battle the fight is shown live, driven by the score: a siege at the gate, a sea battle in the harbour, or Zeus against the rival's god (ZW-018; the siege at the gate was built as a first version on 22 Nov 2026). Later the opponent's city is visible across the water.

## Ideas for each person, inside the shared game

15. **Titles and jobs.** A citizen earns a title and a job from how they take part (chatting, turning up, helping others, supporting). The constitution keeps these as four separate strengths, never one score, so a quiet regular and a big supporter both have a place.
16. **Rare items.** Things that can only be got at a moment: a statue from a victory, a lantern from a festival. "I was there."
17. **Collections.** An album in the builder of everything in the catalogue, filled in as items are unlocked and owned.
18. **Founders and firsts.** The first to build in a new district, the first hundred citizens, the citizen whose pledge completed a build: each recorded on a monument.
19. **Neighbours.** Streets with names, neighbours who can water each other's tree, a street that wins "best kept" each week.
20. **Pets.** An animal that lives at your house, unlocked at a city level (ties in with ZW-003).

## What the constitution already rules in or out

Worth knowing before choosing, since these are the project's own rules:

- **Levels must belong to the city, not to a person's spending.** "Belonging is free; prestige is earned" and "collective over individual". A shared city level fits exactly. A personal level bought with gifts would not.
- **No single personal score.** Standing is four separate strengths, never added up. Personal progress should be titles, jobs and collections, not one number.
- **Nothing earned is ever taken away.** Battle damage has to be a temporary state on top of a building (cracks, scaffolding, scorch marks) that is repaired or fades, not the loss of the building. The constitution's battle section already describes this, including repair as a shared project. ZW-015 fits if built that way.
- **Being away is never punished.** Locks can make people want to come back; nothing may decay because they did not.
- **No money on screen.** Bolts may be shown; coins, gift values and rankings by spend may not.
- **Nobody can do it alone.** No single citizen may supply more than a fifth of a community build.

## Questions for the owner

1. Is one shared city level the right centre for this, with personal progress as titles and collections around it?
2. What should raise the level: mostly Bolts, or a mix that includes people, builds, wins and time?
3. How many levels, and roughly how long should one take: a stream, a week, a month?
4. Which of the four teaser mysteries opens first, and at which level?
5. How much should be visible while locked: a clear silhouette, or only a hint?
6. Should there be seasons that start a new chapter, or one continuous city?
7. Which three or four ideas above are the most exciting to you?

## A possible first slice

If the direction is right, the smallest version that would already change how the stream feels:

- City level with a name, shown on stream with a bar to the next.
- Three levels to begin with, each unlocking one new place and a few plot items.
- Locked plots and one shut gate visible in the city.
- A level-up moment: lightning, a camera move, the gate opening.

Everything else can be added one idea at a time.

## Discussion, 23 Nov 2026: the owner's direction after the research, and proposals in reply

After reading the research the owner set four directions. The proposals under each are ideas offered in reply; none is decided.

### 1. Bolts and slow growth

Owner: "People will have the ability to build based on the Bolts they own and grow slowly. Comments, taps count as well. We will be playing games and whoever wins gets Bolts as well."

Proposals:

- **A home that grows in stages**: tent, hut, cottage, villa, grand villa. Each costs several times the last, so a regular moves up over weeks, not one stream.
- **Games on stream that pay Bolts**, all won by taking part or by skill, never by chance or by paying to enter (TikTok's rules reportedly prohibit games of chance on LIVE):
  - Riddle of the Sphinx: first correct answers in chat.
  - Myth trivia, in rounds.
  - Spot it: something hidden in the city; first to name where.
  - The Games: a foot race or chariot race in the stadium where each citizen's runner is driven by that viewer's own taps.
  - Best garden of the week: the community votes; the winner's street gets a prize.
  - Tug of war between two halves of chat.
- **Caps per stream** on Bolts from taps and watching, as the constitution already says, so growth stays slow and tapping cannot be farmed.

### 2. Showcasing the battle's MVP

Owner: "For MVP showcase, we need to discuss and need your help with some engaging ideas."

Proposals:

- **The triumph.** After a win the hero's citizen rides a chariot through the south gate and up the main street to the plaza, with citizens lining the way and cheering. A queen crowns them with laurel on the palace steps. The camera then follows them home.
- **The house tour.** The camera walks their property with Zeya as guide: how long they have been a citizen, what they built first, the rarest thing they own. Neighbours come out to greet them.
- **The feast.** Their garden hosts the victory party for the rest of the stream: tables, dancing, lanterns.
- **What stays.** A laurel wreath over their door until the next battle, and their name carved on the victory column for good.
- **A postcard.** A still of the hero at their home, framed, that they can share.
- **Three heroes, not one.** To keep faith with the rule against ranking by spend while still honouring the battle's top supporter: the Champion (the battle's MVP, named without amounts), the Voice (who rallied the chat most), and the Herald (who brought the most people in). All three ride in the triumph; one home is toured each time, in turn. The owner decides.

### 3. A structure that builds over time and opens levels

Owner: "Structuring the game so everything builds over time and scales and opens levels."

Proposals:

- **Four ladders, one inside the next.**

  | Ladder | Who climbs it | Example steps |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Home | One person | Tent, hut, cottage, villa, grand villa; garden, furniture, a pet |
  | Street | A dozen neighbours | Dirt lane, paved, lamps, a fountain, a named street with its own banner |
  | City | Everyone | Camp, village, town, city, polis, capital; each opens buildings, events and places |
  | World | Everyone, over months | The land beyond the walls: harbour routes, islands, the four mysteries |

- **Streets** are worth having because research on shared projects found smaller groups more active per person than large ones.
- **Ages.** The whole city changes look as it rises: an age of wood, of stone, of marble, of gold. The catalogue already has stone, ochre and gold-marble walls and four roof grades, so this fits what exists.
- **Targets that scale with the audience**, and bars that never start at zero, both supported by the research.
- **Every level-up is an event**: announced ahead, counted down, with something visibly opening.

### 4. Elevated moments worth waiting for

Owner: "Elevated moments and getting into ranking or winning big showcase something very interesting which people want to see again but have to wait till the momentum happens again."

Proposals:

- **Zeus's favour.** A meter that fills from the whole community's energy: a big battle win, a winning streak, the stream entering a ranking, a record crowd, a level-up. When it fills, a spectacle plays. It cannot be bought directly and it cannot be forced; it needs the moment.
- **Three grades of spectacle**, rarer as they get grander:

  | Grade | Roughly how often | Examples |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Bright | Most streams | Fireworks over the harbour, a rain of gold, doves released from the temple, every lamp lit at once |
  | Rare | A few times a month | Zeus's face in the storm clouds hurling lightning, the fleet sailing in under torchlight, an eagle carrying a wreath to the hero, the colossus turning its head |
  | Legendary | A handful of times ever | The colossus stepping off its plinth and walking the harbour, the sky temple descending through the clouds, the vault opening, the mountain splitting to free a titan |

- **Never the same twice in a row**, drawn from a pool, with some held back unseen so there is always one nobody has witnessed.
- **A hall of moments.** Each spectacle is recorded with its date and the names of everyone present. "I was there" is the reward; it costs nothing and cannot be bought later.
- **Replayed, not lost.** The research on one-time events found that people who missed them resented it. So each is shown again in the next stream's recap and leaves a lasting mark in the city; only being *in* the record requires being there.
- **A care from the research:** the trigger should be the community's momentum from all kinds of taking part. A spectacle that plays when gifts reach a number would read as soliciting gifts, which TikTok's rules reportedly penalise.

### The owner's answers, 23 Nov 2026, and what follows from them

1. **Games:** "Family Feud, Bible quiz, number guessing and so on. Anyone can participate."
2. **MVPs:** "MVPs are top 3 in any battle." So the triumph and the house tour honour the battle's top three, not the three roles proposed above. Recorded as an addendum to the constitution.
3. **The backbone** (four ladders and the ages): the owner is not familiar with how games structure this and leaves it to recommendation.
4. **First spectacle:** "you suggest."

**Giving each game a place in the city.** The games stay exactly as the owner plays them; the city gives them a stage and a result people can see.

| Game | Where it happens | What the city shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family Feud | The amphitheatre | Two families of citizens on stage, the answers revealed one by one on a great board, the crowd reacting; the winning family's street hangs a banner |
| Bible quiz | The library steps | Scholars gathered, a scroll unrolled for each question, the winner handed a laurel |
| Number guessing | The round shrine, as "the oracle's number" | Guesses rise as lanterns; the closest lights up |
| Any game | The plaza | The winner's citizen walks up to receive their Bolts; a small trophy appears at their home |

- A board of games champions in the stadium counts wins at games. It is not a ranking by spend, so it sits comfortably with the constitution.
- Everyone who takes part earns a little; winners earn more. Entry is always free.
- Number guessing is partly luck. With free entry and a prize that exists only in the city it is unlikely to be what TikTok means by games of chance, but the rule should be read on TikTok's own page before relying on that.
- It needs a way for the owner to say who won: a button on a console to begin with, reading answers from chat later.

**The recommended backbone**, in plain terms:

- **Your home grows** as you earn Bolts: tent, hut, cottage, villa, grand villa.
- **Your street grows** when the dozen neighbours on it grow together: paved, lit, a fountain, a name.
- **The city grows** when everyone does: camp, village, town, city, polis, capital. Each step opens new buildings, events and places.
- **The world opens** over months: beyond the walls, the islands, the four mysteries.
- **The city's look changes with its age**: wood, then stone, then marble, then gold. Anyone glancing at the stream can tell how far the community has come.

**The recommended first spectacle: Zeus's storm of gold.** The sky darkens in seconds. Thunder rolls. Zeus's eyes open in the clouds above the temple. Lightning strikes the temple roof again and again until it blazes gold, and a rain of gold falls over the whole city while every citizen stops and cheers and every lamp flares. Then the clouds part to a clear sky. It suits the name, it uses the lightning already built, and it needs no new models. The one to save as the first legendary: the colossus stepping off its plinth.

**The recommended next build: what happens after a battle is won.**

1. The rival is driven off (built).
2. If the moment has earned it, the storm of gold.
3. The triumph: the top three ride through the south gate and up the main street to the plaza, citizens lining the way.
4. The house tour: the camera follows the first of them home and walks the property with their name on screen.
5. A wreath over their door, their names on the victory column.

All on made-up names for now, as the battle is. It turns the battle into a full story with an ending people want to be in.
